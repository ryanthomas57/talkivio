# Metrics dictionary

_Generated by `apps/api/scripts/metrics-dictionary.mjs` from the running code (analytics definitions version 1). Do not edit by hand._

## Product analytics measures

Every analytics response carries `definitions_version`, the population and sample size. Times are UTC; report boundaries are half-open `[from, to)` in the report time zone.

| Key | Label | Kind | Unit | Population | Definition | Null / empty policy |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `requested` | Requested chats | count | chats | Chats with requested_at in [from, to), preview chats excluded | Count of admitted chat requests. | zero_when_no_events |
| `assigned` | Assigned chats | count | chats | Chats with requested_at in [from, to), preview chats excluded | Chats with first_assigned_at set. Assigned is not served. | zero_when_no_events |
| `served` | Served chats | count | chats | Chats with requested_at in [from, to), preview chats excluded | Chats with at least one human agent public message (automated messages and internal notes excluded). | zero_when_no_events |
| `missed` | Missed chats | count | chats | Chats with requested_at in [from, to), preview chats excluded | Closed chats without a human public response after admission. Overlaps abandoned. | `abandoned` | Abandoned chats | count | chats | Chats with requested_at in [from, to), preview chats excluded | Chats the visitor ended, or whose reconnect grace expired, before first assignment. May overlap missed; never add the two. | zero_when_no_events |
| `quick_cancellations` | Quick visitor cancellations | count | chats | Chats with requested_at in [from, to), preview chats excluded | Visitor ended the chat within 10 s of requesting it, before assignment (disclosed separately). | zero_when_no_events |

## Operational metrics (Prometheus)

Exposed on `/health/metrics` (api/gateway) and the worker health listener (`WORKER_HEALTH_PORT`), internal network or `METRICS_TOKEN` only. Alert rules: `infra/observability/alert-rules.yml`; runbooks: `docs/operations/runbooks.md`.

| Metric | Type | Labels | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `talkivio_commands_total` | counter | `command`, `outcome` | Commands by outcome (error budget). |
| `talkivio_http_request_duration_seconds` | histogram | `method`, `route_group`, `status_class` | REST request duration. |
| `talkivio_message_delivery_seconds` | histogram | — | Commit to socket emit for durable events. |
| `talkivio_outbox_oldest_unpublished_seconds` | gauge | — | Age of the oldest unpublished outbox row. Derived from PostgreSQL at scrape time (5 s cache). |
| `talkivio_routing_queue_oldest_seconds` | gauge | `routable` | Age of the oldest queued chat. Derived from PostgreSQL at scrape time (5 s cache). `routable=true`: an eligible online agent exists; `false`: expected outside hours. |
| `talkivio_routing_offer_seconds` | histogram | `mode` | Routing pass duration that created an offer. |
| `talkivio_sockets_connected` | gauge | `namespace` | Connected sockets on this node. |
| `talkivio_socket_overloads_total` | counter | `namespace` | Slow consumers closed for exceeding the outbound buffer (1000 events / 1 MiB). |
| `talkivio_socket_reconnects_total` | counter | `namespace` | Socket connections established (reconnect storms show as spikes). |
| `talkivio_job_failures_total` | counter | `queue` | Failed background jobs. |
| `talkivio_job_duration_seconds` | histogram | `queue` | Background job duration. Buckets: 0.01, 0.05, 0.1, 0.5, 1, 5, 15, 60, 300 s. |
| `talkivio_scan_duration_seconds` | histogram | `verdict` | Malware scan duration. Buckets: 0.05, 0.1, 0.5, 1, 2, 5, 10, 30, 60 s. |
| `talkivio_scan_backlog_seconds` | gauge | — | Age of the oldest file awaiting a scan verdict. Derived from PostgreSQL at scrape time (5 s cache). |
| `talkivio_email_deliveries_total` | counter | `outcome` | Outbound email attempts. |
| `talkivio_webhook_deliveries_total` | counter | `outcome`, `status_class` | Webhook delivery attempts. |
| `talkivio_monitor_freshness_seconds` | gauge | — | Age of the newest monitor snapshot pushed. |

Content never appears in metrics: labels are route groups, outcomes, queues and namespaces — never ids, emails or message text.
